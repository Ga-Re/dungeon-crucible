# Renderer benchmark – 2026-11-23, run 1 (living fog vs. still fog)

**Setup:** DM's Mac (Apple GPU), table TV 3840×2160 at 58.8 Hz, devicePixelRatio 1. `npm run bench`
(ported from Dungeon-Manager T-105d): 4K H.264 video map (synthetic, 30 fps, from the campaign
folder via the asset protocol), fog with holes, 20 tokens, moving camera. 5 s warm-up, 30 s
measurement. A frame misses a refresh when its interval exceeds 1.5 × the measured refresh interval.

| Fog | FPS | p50 | p95 | p99 | max | Missed refreshes | Dropped video frames¹ | Budget (≤ 1 %) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Living fog, quality Mittel (default) | 59.97 | 17 ms | 18 ms | 18 ms | 48 ms | 4 / 1800 (0.22 %) | 1190 | met |
| Still fog with soft edges (`VITE_BENCH_QUALITY=off`) | 59.93 | 17 ms | 18 ms | 20 ms | 49 ms | 3 / 1798 (0.17 %) | 1190 | met |

¹ WebKit's counter: the same value in both runs and more than the video's 900 frames in 30 s, so
it does not describe this scene (as in Dungeon-Manager's T-007 notes: indicative only). The video's
smoothness has to be judged by eye on the TV.

**Findings**

- Both configurations meet the 60 FPS budget at 4K with a 4K video map.
- The living fog (Mittel) costs nothing measurable on this machine: Mittel stays the default, also
  with video maps.
- The few missed refreshes fall at the same moments in both runs (≈ 1.15 s and ≈ 9.0 s into the
  measurement), so they are not caused by the fog.
